# Privacy Policy — Wreckday: Lost Island

**Effective Date:** October 30, 2026

Wreckday: Lost Island ("the Game") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed, how it is used, and the choices available to you when playing the Game.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Game may collect and process the following information to provide its features:

**Gameplay Content**
- Game-related information necessary for gameplay functionality.
- Virtual currency (Shells) balances and wallet transaction history.
- Records of in-game rewards and purchases.

**User Identifiers**
- A unique wallet identifier used to associate and synchronize virtual currency records.
- Game Center player identifiers where applicable.

**Purchase History**
- In-app purchase product identifiers.
- Transaction identifiers used to verify purchases, prevent duplicate rewards, and maintain accurate Shells balances.

**Product Interaction**
- Information about the use of in-game items, cards, rewards, and virtual currency where needed for wallet accounting.

Some gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. Wallet information may be synchronized through Apple's CloudKit service.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively for application functionality, including:

- Providing and maintaining gameplay features.
- Processing and verifying in-app purchases.
- Maintaining virtual currency balances.
- Preventing duplicate transactions.
- Synchronizing eligible wallet data through iCloud.
- Supporting Game Center leaderboards.
- Investigating user-reported issues.

We do not use this information for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 3. Apple Services

The Game integrates the following Apple services:

**Apple iCloud / CloudKit:** Used to store and synchronize eligible virtual currency records associated with your iCloud account.

**Apple StoreKit:** Used to process in-app purchases. Payment information, such as credit card or bank account details, is handled by Apple. The Game does not receive your payment card details.

**Apple Game Center:** Used for player authentication and leaderboards. Your Game Center display name and leaderboard scores may be visible to other players according to Apple's Game Center functionality and your account settings.

Apple's handling of information is also subject to the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 4. Data Sharing and Advertising

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share gameplay or wallet information with advertising networks or data brokers.

Information is processed through Apple services as necessary for the Game to function, and may be disclosed where legally required.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Local gameplay data is stored on your device. Eligible wallet data is stored using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure.

We use transaction verification, persistent records, and other safeguards designed to protect wallet integrity and prevent duplicate virtual currency transactions.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained as necessary to provide Game functionality, maintain wallet records, address support requests, and comply with applicable obligations.

Deleting the Game may remove locally stored gameplay progress but does not necessarily delete information stored in iCloud or purchase records maintained by Apple.

You can contact us to request information about data associated with the Game or ask about available deletion options.

Certain records may need to be retained where required for transaction integrity or legal compliance.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, as well as other rights under applicable privacy laws.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address below. We will review your request in accordance with applicable requirements.

You can also manage certain iCloud and Game Center information through your Apple account settings.

## 8. Customer Support

If you voluntarily contact support, we may receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

Support communications are used to respond to your request and investigate reported problems.

When you prepare a bug report, the Game includes technical information such as the app version and build number, iOS version, device model, current game day, health, hunger, overheating, and selected resource values. This information is displayed in the report and is transmitted only if you choose to send or share it. We use it solely to investigate the reported issue.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
