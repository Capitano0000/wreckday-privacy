Frequently Asked Questions

How can I report a bug?

Use the REPORT A BUG button in the game, or email us. Include the app version, device model, and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

I have a problem with Shells purchases.

Check your internet connection and restart the game. If purchased Shells are still missing, contact support with the approximate purchase date and package. For Apple billing or refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com .

My Shells balance is not syncing.

Make sure you're signed in to the correct iCloud account and connected to the internet. If the problem persists, contact support.

My Game Center leaderboard is not working.

Verify that you're signed in to Game Center in your device settings and connected to the internet.

Can I play offline?

Core gameplay is available offline. In-app purchases, Game Center features, and cloud synchronization require an internet connection.
